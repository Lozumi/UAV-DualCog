Platform Extension & Construction

The common bridge preserves the benchmark contract across reconstructed scenes

Step 1

Metric scene ingestion

CityGaussianV2 provides the reconstructed appearance. GauUscene V2.1 contributes metric COLMAP cameras and LiDAR geometry. A scene-specific similarity transform aligns camera poses, LiDAR points, and Gaussian coordinates without retraining the released reconstruction.

Step 2

Common bridge adaptation

The 3DGS adapter implements the same pose-driven capture interface used by the benchmark pipeline. It exposes RGB, depth, alpha, camera projection, and geometry queries so downstream construction code remains independent of the rendering backend.

Step 3

Candidate landmark mining

We sample 200 spatially distributed registered views per scene, segment visually distinctive objects, lift masks through metric depth, and fuse observations in 3D. Spatial deduplication produces 100 candidate landmarks per scene for review.

Step 4

Multiview review

Candidates are checked for visual clarity, cross-view identity, category coverage, minimum view support, projected-box quality, and canonical orientation. The retained 30 landmarks span buildings, vegetation, infrastructure, and urban facilities.

Step 5

Task generation

The shared DCCS image module reuses registered observations and geometry-derived relations to generate four high-difficulty 8-way tasks. Landmark use is balanced where feasible, and every sample follows the same answer-plus-BBox output contract as the AirSim benchmark.

Step 6

Release audit

The final 400 questions are checked for valid media, nondegenerate boxes, option consistency, landmark coverage, and deterministic pair identities. Results remain a separate reconstruction pilot rather than being mixed into the simulation leaderboard.

Analysis

Real-scene reconstruction preserves the benchmark's main capability separation

Marginal strength does not imply paired success

GLM-5.3-Flash reaches the highest four-task macro semantic accuracy (42.25%), yet its Overall Dual Semantic Acc is only 13.64%. Gemini 3.8 Flash similarly reaches 14.35% paired semantics, confirming that independently useful answers rarely become coordinated Agent-World success.

Semantics and localization remain distinct

Gemini 3.8 Flash leads Overall Dual Evidence Acc (69.57%) but reaches only 14.35% Overall Dual Semantic Acc. The gap echoes the AirSim benchmark: recognizing the relation and localizing the shared landmark are complementary capabilities.

What the extension establishes

The result supports extending SUSP/DCCS-style construction and the evaluation contract to 3DGS reconstructions derived from real aerial imagery and metric geometry. It does not establish robustness to physical UAV dynamics, online perception, reconstruction gaps, or out-of-distribution flight viewpoints.
