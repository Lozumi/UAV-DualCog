Platform Extension & Construction

The common simulation bridge now supports a metric-aligned 3DGS scene

Scene ingestion and rendering

The platform loads the released CityGaussianV2 reconstruction through a headless gsplat service. A robust similarity alignment maps GauUscene V2.1 LiDAR and COLMAP coordinates into the Gaussian coordinate system, while rendered depth is converted back to meters for downstream geometry checks.

Semantic landmark construction

Two hundred views per scene are sampled across the registered camera coverage. Open-vocabulary detection and mask segmentation are lifted through metric depth, fused across views, deduplicated in 3D, and ranked by view coverage, confidence, geometric quality, stability, category diversity, and scene coverage.

DualCog task generation

Each visually preselected five-landmark set is passed through the common Stage 4 generator used by the main benchmark. Registered aerial views provide the observations, while geometry-derived labels and projected boxes preserve the same option-plus-BBox output contracts across all four image tasks.

Landmark Review

Each full-scene candidate pool precedes a temporary, spatially diverse selection

Each scene contributes 100 candidates and a visually preselected five-landmark set. Across all scenes, the pools span 68 occupied grids, while the provisional selections span 15 grids pending manual review.

Candidate masks, projected boxes, multiview crops, 3D centers, dimensions, category labels, and quality fields are retained in the review bundle. Human reviewers can keep, revise, merge, or reject each instance before the pilot set is treated as a released annotation.

The temporary selections prioritize visually clear, spatially separated buildings, public facilities, transport infrastructure, and urban landscape elements. Their names and descriptions have been corrected after image review, while final acceptance remains subject to the project author's confirmation.

Analysis Plan

Protocol portability is evaluated separately from model performance

Construction analysis checks alignment residuals, landmark stability, BBox projection, image quality, and manual-review acceptance. Once model results are available, the pilot will report the same image semantic and evidence metrics as UAV-DualCog and use only descriptive comparison with AirSim; no strict sim-to-real performance gap will be inferred from these reconstructed scenes.

A preliminary free-viewpoint smoke test exposed visible artifacts outside the reconstruction's well-observed camera manifold. We therefore retain registered aerial views for image QA and defer video-task construction until a trajectory strategy can satisfy the same visual-quality gate.
